# Ramirez 2012

[источник] Kelly S. Ramirez, Joseph M. Craine, Noah Fierer, «Consistent effects of nitrogen amendments on soil microbial communities and processes across biomes», Global Change Biology, том 18, выпуск 6, страницы 1918–1927, 2012.
[источник] Аннотация: Abstract Ecosystems worldwide are receiving increasing amounts of reactive nitrogen (N) via anthropogenic activities with the added N having potentially important impacts on microbially mediated belowground carbon dynamics. However, a comprehensive understanding of how elevated N availability affects soil microbial processes and community dynamics remains incomplete.
